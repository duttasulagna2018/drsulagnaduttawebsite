Areas of care

Newborn intensive care is Dr Dutta's specialty. She also sees babies and children for routine checks, vaccines and illness.

  • Neonatal intensive care

    Ventilation (HFOV, SIMV), CPAP, high flow and inhaled nitric oxide for babies who need help breathing.

  • Preterm and low birth weight babies

    From the first hours in the NICU to feeding, growth, eye and hearing checks, and the follow-up clinic.

  • Heart and circulation

    Bedside echocardiography and point of care ultrasound to guide treatment of shock, PDA and pulmonary hypertension.

  • Newborn brain care

    Therapeutic hypothermia, amplitude integrated EEG, seizure management and neurodevelopmental follow-up.

  • Feeding and nutrition

    Breastfeeding support, donor milk, fortification and parenteral nutrition for babies with complex needs.

  • Infections and vaccines

    Careful antibiotic use for neonatal sepsis, and the full IAP immunisation schedule for infants and children.

  • Growth and development

    Well baby visits, growth charts, milestones and early help when something seems slow.

  • Procedures

    PICC and umbilical lines, surfactant, exchange transfusion, chest and peritoneal drains, done at the bedside.

When to see a neonatologist

  • Your baby was born before 37 weeks or weighed under 2.5 kg
  • Your baby needed the NICU and now needs follow-up of growth, eyes, hearing and development
  • You are expecting a baby with a known problem on the antenatal scan and want to plan the birth
  • Jaundice, feeding trouble or poor weight gain in the first weeks

Everyday paediatrics

  • Well baby and well child visits, growth and milestones
  • Vaccines on the IAP schedule
  • Fever, cough, diarrhoea, rashes and the illnesses of the monsoon
  • Feeding, sleep and behaviour questions
